OFFICIAL APP SUPPORT

One More Try: Bounce

Need help with gameplay, installation, ads, purchases, Game Center, or another technical issue? Contact Bald Mind Technologies at admin@baldmindtechnologies.com.

When contacting support

Please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A short description of what happened
  • A screenshot or screen recording, when helpful

Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information.

How to play

Tap the opening screen to begin. Tap while the ball is on the ground to jump over obstacles. Airborne taps do not change the ball’s trajectory. The environment changes every 10 points, and score milestones unlock shields and visual rewards.

Ads and continues

An optional rewarded ad may provide one continue per run. Regular interstitial ads may appear between runs; they do not interrupt active gameplay.

Remove Ads and restore purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple. If you previously purchased it, use Restore Purchases in the game. For refund assistance, visit Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Game Center

If the leaderboard does not appear, confirm that Game Center is enabled and signed in under your iPhone settings, then reopen the game.

Privacy

Read the Bald Mind Technologies Privacy Policy, including the One More Try: Bounce privacy disclosure.

Response time

We aim to respond to support messages within two business days.

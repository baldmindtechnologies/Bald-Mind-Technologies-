APP SUPPORT

How can we help?

Official support for Bald Mind Technologies products.

Streak Runner

For gameplay, Game Center, purchase, installation, or technical questions, email admin@baldmindtechnologies.com.

Please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A short description of the problem
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue

Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information.

Purchases and refunds

Streak Runner is a one-time paid download. Apple processes App Store purchases and refund requests. For purchase assistance, visit Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If leaderboards do not appear, confirm that Game Center is enabled and signed in under your iPhone settings, then reopen Streak Runner.

Privacy

Read the Bald Mind Technologies Privacy Policy, including the Streak Runner privacy disclosure.

Response time

We aim to respond to support messages within two business days.

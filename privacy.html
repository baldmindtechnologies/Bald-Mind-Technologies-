Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Bald Mind Technologies LLC respects your privacy. This policy applies to our website and applications, including Streak Runner and One More Try: Bounce. We design our products to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary.

Streak Runner

Streak Runner does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, tracking technology, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Bald Mind Technologies does not collect personal information through Streak Runner.

Scores, earned coins, selected difficulty, unlocked themes, and daily-challenge progress are stored locally on your device. Streak Runner optionally uses Apple Game Center for authentication and leaderboards. Apple processes Game Center information under Apple’s own privacy policy and account settings. The game remains playable without Game Center.

The Share Score feature opens Apple’s system share sheet. Streak Runner does not receive or retain the destination you select or the content handled by another application.

One More Try: Bounce

One More Try: Bounce does not require a Bald Mind Technologies account. The game stores the local best score, gameplay progress, purchase status, and unlocked cosmetic rewards on the device.

The game optionally uses Apple Game Center for authentication, leaderboards, and achievements. Apple processes Game Center information under Apple’s privacy policy and account settings.

One More Try: Bounce uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage applicable privacy choices. Depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, advertising data, product interaction, and diagnostic information to deliver, limit, measure, and protect advertising. Bald Mind Technologies does not use this information to create its own personally identifiable player profile. For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy.

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed through Apple StoreKit. Bald Mind Technologies receives transaction status needed to unlock the purchase but does not receive your payment-card information. Apple processes purchase information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Information You Provide

If you contact us directly, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

Website Data

Hosting, domain, and security providers may process limited technical data such as IP address, browser information, and request logs to operate and protect the website.

Cookies

This site does not intentionally use advertising or tracking cookies.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to operate, secure, or communicate through the website.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or deletion requests can be sent to admin@baldmindtechnologies.com or through the contact section. Because our games do not maintain a Bald Mind Technologies account or server-side player profile, there may be no game account data for us to delete.

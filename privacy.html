Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Bald Mind Technologies LLC respects your privacy. This policy applies to our website and applications, including Brothers in Christ, Streak Runner, and One More Try: Bounce. We design our products to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary.

Streak Runner

Streak Runner does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, tracking technology, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Bald Mind Technologies does not collect personal information through Streak Runner.

Scores, earned coins, selected difficulty, unlocked themes, and daily-challenge progress are stored locally on your device. Streak Runner optionally uses Apple Game Center for authentication and leaderboards. Apple processes Game Center information under Apple’s own privacy policy and account settings. The game remains playable without Game Center.

The Share Score feature opens Apple’s system share sheet. Streak Runner does not receive or retain the destination you select or the content handled by another application.

One More Try: Bounce

One More Try: Bounce does not require a Bald Mind Technologies account. The game stores the local best score, gameplay progress, purchase status, and unlocked cosmetic rewards on the device.

The game optionally uses Apple Game Center for authentication, leaderboards, and achievements. Apple processes Game Center information under Apple’s privacy policy and account settings.

One More Try: Bounce uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage applicable privacy choices. Depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, advertising data, product interaction, and diagnostic information to deliver, limit, measure, and protect advertising. Bald Mind Technologies does not use this information to create its own personally identifiable player profile. For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy.

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed through Apple StoreKit. Bald Mind Technologies receives transaction status needed to unlock the purchase but does not receive your payment-card information. Apple processes purchase information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Brothers in Christ

Brothers in Christ version 1.1 is available through the iPhone and iPad app, Android app, and companion website. It includes the KJV Bible, native study and application tools, words-of-Jesus red lettering, Scripture highlights, local notes, channel videos, optional notifications, and public or private prayer requests.

Bible study, highlights, and private notes

Bible-reading activity, Scripture highlights, highlighted-verse history, bookmarks, and personal study notes are stored locally on your device or browser. Bald Mind Technologies does not upload this private study information. The Bible, available study material, highlights, and notes may remain accessible when the device is offline.

Prayer requests and prayer reactions

If you submit a prayer request, we receive the request text and any optional first name or Scripture reference you enter. Public requests appear on the prayer board only after administrator approval. Private requests are available only in the ministry administrator inbox and are never posted publicly.

The app and website create a random, app-specific device identifier used to limit spam, repeated prayer submissions, and duplicate prayer reactions. When you tap the 🙏 reaction, that identifier may be used to maintain the synchronized reaction status and public reaction total across the website and apps. It is not displayed publicly and is not used by Bald Mind Technologies to identify you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Prayer requests may be retained until the ministry administrator marks them handled, archives them, or deletes them. Items moved to Deleted may be retained for up to 14 days to permit recovery before permanent removal. You may request deletion through the contact information below. Do not include phone numbers, addresses, medical records, or other sensitive identifying information in a public request.

Video and live-stream notifications

If you enable notifications, the app sends an Apple or Google notification token and your selected preferences for new videos, Shorts, and live streams to the Brothers in Christ notification service. These tokens and preferences are used only to register the device, deliver the notifications you request, maintain delivery security, and prevent abuse. They are not used by Bald Mind Technologies for advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can separately turn video/Short and live-stream notifications on or off in the app’s Settings screen. You can also disable all Brothers in Christ notifications through your device’s system settings. Apple and Google may process information necessary to deliver notifications under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and consent

The iPhone, iPad, and Android apps use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform to display a compact banner ad on the Home and Watch screens and to manage applicable privacy choices. Ads are not displayed in Bible reading, Study & Apply, private notes, or prayer. Depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, advertising data, product interaction, consent information, crash data, and performance data to deliver, limit, measure, secure, and protect advertising. Bald Mind Technologies does not use this information to create its own personally identifiable user profile. For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy. Advertising privacy choices are available in app settings when required.

YouTube and other services

The apps and website display information from the official Brothers in Christ YouTube channel and provide links to videos and live streams. YouTube links may open YouTube or your browser. Your use of YouTube is governed by Google’s privacy policy. Apple and Google process information needed for app distribution, device services, and notification delivery under their respective policies.

Children

Brothers in Christ is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise a child’s use of prayer-submission and public prayer-board features.

Information You Provide

If you contact us directly, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

Website Data

Hosting, domain, security, and content-delivery providers may process limited technical data such as IP address, browser information, device information, and request logs to operate, secure, diagnose, and protect the websites and services.

Cookies

This site does not intentionally use advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services opened from our websites, such as YouTube or Google, operate under their own cookie and privacy practices.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to operate, secure, support, or communicate through our websites and applications, deliver requested notifications, moderate prayer submissions, or provide advertising described in this policy.

Your Choices and Contact

You can stop future app-based collection by disabling optional features or deleting the app. Deleting the app does not automatically delete a prayer request that was previously submitted to the ministry.

Questions, privacy requests, or deletion requests can be sent to admin@baldmindtechnologies.com or through the contact section. Because our games do not maintain a Bald Mind Technologies account or server-side player profile, there may be no game account data for us to delete. Brothers in Christ prayer requests can be reviewed for deletion through the same contact address.

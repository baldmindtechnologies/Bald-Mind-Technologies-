BROTHERS IN CHRIST · APP SUPPORT

How can we help?

Official support for the Brothers In Christ iPhone app, published by Bald Mind Technologies LLC.

Contact support

Email admin@baldmindtechnologies.com.

Please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The Brothers In Christ app version
  • A short description of the problem
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue

Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, medical records, or private prayer information as part of a technical support request.

Bible, study, notes, and appearance

The complete King James Version and personal notes are available in the app. Notes remain stored on your device. Use the gear button to choose System, Light, or Dark appearance.

If Bible or study content does not appear correctly, close and reopen the app. If the issue continues, restart the iPhone and include the affected book, chapter, or verse in your support message.

Videos and live streams

The Home and Watch screens display videos from the official @BIC_Real YouTube channel. Pull down on the Watch screen to refresh. Video and live-stream links may open YouTube or your browser.

Prayer requests

Public prayer requests are reviewed by the ministry administrator before appearing on the public prayer board. Private requests are visible only to the ministry administrator.

To request correction or deletion of a submitted prayer request, email admin@baldmindtechnologies.com. Include enough non-sensitive detail to locate the request, but do not resend confidential information unnecessarily.

Advertising and privacy choices

Banner advertising is limited to the Home and Watch screens. Bible reading, study, notes, and prayer remain ad-free. Ads are capped at Google’s G digital-content rating.

When required in your region, advertising privacy choices are available from the app’s Settings screen. Read the Brothers In Christ privacy disclosures.

Response time

We aim to respond to support messages within two business days.
